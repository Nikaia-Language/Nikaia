in their current form. They are kept for one purpose: to show **how** a
decision was reached - the options weighed then, the measurements of the day,
the route. Nothing here is normative and nothing outside `docs/history/` cites
a record here (`scripts/check-adr-refs.py` refuses it). For the decision itself,
read the record in the right-hand column.

# ADR-190: The Tether Is Needed At An Escape And Nowhere Else — `?.` Has No Escape In It

**Status:** Superseded by [ADR-278](../../specification/adr/adr-278.md) — its D1 and D4 are part of D17, its D2 is answered by D20, its D3 is part of D19 there
**Date:** September 22, 2026
**Target Version:** Nikaia 0.0.141
**Corrects the reason of:** [ADR-189](adr-189.md) D3 and §4, whose conclusion
stands and whose stated cause does not — the record is not rewritten
**Related:** [ADR-008](adr-008.md) D2 (the lattice, and what *escapes* means),
[ADR-113](adr-113.md) D2 (a reached member that does not copy is a view),
[ADR-156](adr-156.md) D4 (`NK2303`, the refusal that stands where Tethered
would)

## 1. Context

[ADR-189](adr-189.md) put the third of `?.` — a member that does not copy —
behind [#196](https://github.com/Nikaia-Language/Nikaia/issues/196), the tether. The owner asked
the right question about that: *the tether is only needed at very specific
boundaries, isn't it?*

It is, and [ADR-008](adr-008.md) D2 says so in its own table: **Tethered is
chosen when the value escapes the buffer's owning scope**, which means returned
past it, captured by a `@detached` lambda, or written into a longer-lived
container or a global. A struct field is not an escape by itself — that sentence
is what makes the 1BRC inner loop free.

None of that is a `?.`.

## 2. Decisions

### D1 — Measured: three of the four shapes need no state at all.

The lowering [ADR-113](adr-113.md) D2 asks for is
`x.as_ref().map(|it| &it.a)`. Against this repository's toolchain:

| shape | what happens | state |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| receiver is a **local**, the view stays in its scope | compiles, runs | **Borrowed** |
| receiver is a **temporary**, the view is consumed in the same statement | compiles, runs | **Borrowed** |
| view **`??` view** — `user?.name ?? "nobody"`, and a literal *is* a view | compiles, runs, zero cost | **Borrowed** |
| view **bound past a temporary** — `let n = find(1)?.name` | `rustc`: *temporary value dropped while borrowed* | an **escape** |

Only the last is an escape, and it is not Tethered either: it is the case
`NK2303` already covers one construct over — a view of a buffer that does not
outlive it, refused in this language's words and naming `.to_owned()`
([ADR-156](adr-156.md) D4). This compiler already builds that answer twice.

**So no part of `?.` needs the representation.** [ADR-189](adr-189.md)'s §4 said
it waits on [#196](https://github.com/Nikaia-Language/Nikaia/issues/196) with *everything else that wants a state between Borrowed and
Owned*, and that sentence is what this record takes back.

### D2 — What actually blocks it is one question about `??`.

A view on the left of a `??` and an **owned** value on the right:
`user?.name ?? "nobody".to_owned()`. Measured, the two cannot be joined:

* an owned result needs a copy on the borrowed branch, and a compiler-inserted
  copy is what [ADR-008](adr-008.md) D5 forbids outright;
* a view result needs the fallback to be one, which `"nobody"` already is and
  `"nobody".to_owned()` is not;
* refusing the mixed form names `.to_owned()`, which the program already wrote.

That is a decision about what `??` means over a view, not a representation, and
it is the owner's: it decides whether ~6 sites in
`crates/nikaia/tests/nullable.rs` drop a `.to_owned()` they only ever needed
because the left side was owned. It goes to
[`open-decisions.md`](../../open-decisions.md) in that page's shape.

### D3 — [ADR-189](adr-189.md) D3's conclusion stands; its reason is replaced.

The third case still lowers as it did and is still **not refused** — that half
was right, and for the reason it gave: refusing `find(1)?.name ?? "nobody".to_owned()`
would refuse a program that compiles and runs today
([C.4](../../specification/30-nikaia-tooling.md)). What changes is the *cause* written beside it:
it waits on D2's answer, not on a representation.

**The record is not rewritten** ([ADR-187](adr-187.md) D4's rule, met a second
time inside one day): a record says what was decided when, and the correction
goes to the living sentences and to the index's *superseded and narrowed* table.

### D4 — What the tether is still needed for is written down, so this is not read as *not at all*.

[ADR-008](adr-008.md)'s own worked check names the one shape in that program:
**a `Summary` returned past `main`'s scope** — a container of views outliving
the buffer they point into. That, a parser handing its rows past the buffer's
scope, and a `@detached` lambda capturing one are what Tethered is for.

**And nothing in the tree is one**, which the analysis has already measured:
every view in `examples/`, `benches/` and `tests/` solves to Borrowed, and
`crates/nikaia/tests/tethers.rs` holds that as a ceiling over the whole corpus.

## 3. Consequences

* **[#192](https://github.com/Nikaia-Language/Nikaia/issues/192) stops pointing at the largest entry in the file.** It
  is one `??` decision away from finished, not four change packages.
* **[#196](https://github.com/Nikaia-Language/Nikaia/issues/196)'s *what rests on it* loses a line it gained the same day.** Two of the
  three things that list has named turned out not to rest on it — the grammar
  entry's `keeps` at 0.0.137, and this. What is left on it is *text is one type*
  and `Bytes`.
* **The method this cost.** [ADR-189](adr-189.md) reasoned from D2's wording —
  *a view of the receiver* — to D2's **state**, without asking which state. The
  lattice has three, the cheap one is the default, and *view* says nothing about
  which. A shape's state is a measurement, the same rule
  [ADR-187](adr-187.md) had just been written about one day earlier.
* **What it costs:** nothing built changes. This is a reason corrected and a
  question filed.

## 4. What this does not decide

* **What `??` means over a view.** D2's question, on
  [`open-decisions.md`](../../open-decisions.md).
* **Anything about the tether itself.** [#196](https://github.com/Nikaia-Language/Nikaia/issues/196) stands exactly as it is, minus one
  entry that was never behind it.
* **Whether `?.` over a non-copy member should yield a view at all.** It should
  — [ADR-113](adr-113.md) D2 decided that, and this record only says what the
  answer costs.

# ADR-141: Six Things the Specification Writes Are Corrections, and Three Are Constructs That Stay Unspecified

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** September 19, 2026
**Related:** [ADR-278](adr-278.md) (`null` and the `T?`), [ADR-025](adr-025.md) D2 (a failure leaves without a sigil), [ADR-023](adr-023.md) D1 (an error type is an `enum`), Part I 4.4, 6.6, 7.1 and 8, `docs/language-review.md` §3.5

## 1. Context

`language-review.md` §3.5 lists nine things the specification writes that the
language does not have. They are not one kind of thing, and telling the two
kinds apart is the whole of this record.

**Six are slips**: a page reaching for a surface the language deliberately does
not have, or writing a type by a name it does not carry. Each has a correct
spelling that exists, so each is a page edit with no ruling behind it.

**Three are constructs**: a duration literal, a channel and a `select` block.
Each is a feature the language would gain and belongs to Part I chapter 8, and
none had a record. A page that writes one is not a slip — it is a page ahead
of a decision.

## 2. Decisions

### D1 — The six are corrected on the page, and each correction is the spelling that exists.

*Changed by [ADR-328](adr-328.md): the call is `supervisor::run`, with `restart:` and `policy:` per child.*

| written | is |
| :--- | :--- |
| `neg.is_some()` (`examples/1brc.nika`) | `neg != null` — there is no `Some` here ([ADR-278](adr-278.md)) |
| `io::read_to_string("input.txt")?` (Part III C.4) | `io::read_to_string("input.txt")` — a failure leaves on its own ([ADR-025](adr-025.md) D2) |
| `List[Token]`, `List[Row]`, `List[Fortune]` | `Vec[…]` — Part I 2.2's prose calls the concept *List (Vector)* and the type is `Vec` |
| `panic::on_panic fn(info) sync { … }` | the hook without the word: a lambda carries no promise ([ADR-277](adr-277.md) D6 puts it on the *parameter's type*) |
| `supervisor::start_link(…; restart_policy: "always")` | an `enum`, which is what Part I 4.4 argues for where a string names one of a few things |
| `TimeoutError("Too slow!")` | an `enum` variant, because an error type is an `enum` ([ADR-023](adr-023.md) D1) |

The first three are wrong about a **spelling**; the last three are wrong about
a **shape**, and each of those three is a page writing the thing the page next
to it argues against.

### D2 — The three constructs are marked *unspecified*, in place.

*Changed by [ADR-150](adr-150.md) D2, [ADR-149](adr-149.md) D1 and [ADR-292](adr-292.md) D12: each of the three is now decided by its own record, and its page carries that record's rule rather than the mark.*

A construct written on a page with no record behind it is marked
**unspecified** in place (D3), not removed and not decided in passing: removing
it loses the shape somebody wrote down and the argument it was making, and
deciding it in a footnote takes a language feature without a record's worth of
question — what a duration *is* as a type, whether a channel is `std`'s or the
language's, and what `select` does to `sync`.

### D3 — The mark is a form, and the specification gets one.

> **Unspecified.** This writes a construct the language does not have and no
> record decides. It is here for the shape of the example; the decision is
> named where it will be taken.

One paragraph in [`specification/README.md`](../README.md), beside the
**Status** note's own definition, so that a reader who meets the mark knows
what it promises: nothing, yet, and where to look. The two marks differ: a
**Status** note stands under a decided rule the compiler has not caught up
with, the **Unspecified** mark under a construct nobody decided, and a page may
carry both, one under the other.

## 3. Consequences

* Six examples stop teaching a surface the language does not have — and the
  three that are wrong about a shape stop contradicting the page beside them.
  `List[…]` becomes `Vec` wherever a page writes it.
* A reader who meets a construct ahead of its decision is told so rather than
  left to find out by compiling it.
* **What it costs:** six edits and one paragraph of convention.

## 4. What this does not decide

* **The duration literal**, **the channel** and **`select`** — decided by
  [ADR-150](adr-150.md), [ADR-149](adr-149.md) and [ADR-292](adr-292.md), not
  here.
* **Whether the specification's other unbuilt examples get the mark.** They
  are ahead of the *compiler* and their records exist, which is a different
  state and has the **Status** note it already has.
